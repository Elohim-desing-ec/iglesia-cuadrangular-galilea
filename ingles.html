Who we are

A family of faith in Milagro, Ecuador

Iglesia Cuadrangular Galilea is part of the Foursquare Gospel Church of Ecuador. We held our first official meeting on July 4, 2018 in the Las Piñas neighborhood of Milagro, and were officially established as a church in November 2019. Our heart is to see lives transformed, families restored and a generation that impacts the world with the gospel.

Our pastors

Victor H. Rivera & Daniela Macías Valle

Location

Milagro, Guayas, Ecuador

Service times

Join us this week

Everyone is welcome, wherever you are in your journey of faith. All services are held in Spanish (Ecuador time, GMT-5).

Sunday Service

Sunday · 10:00 AM

Worship, the Word and fellowship for the whole family in our main sanctuary.

Fasting & Prayer

1st Sunday of the month · 1:00 PM

A time set apart to seek God's presence in fasting and prayer.

Prayer Service

Monday · 7:30 PM

We gather to intercede and seek God together in prayer.

Women & Men

Tuesday · 7:30 PM

Women's and men's services alternate every other week throughout the month.

Couples

Last Tuesday of the month · 7:30 PM

A special time to strengthen marriages, once a month.

Bible Study

Wednesday · 7:30 PM

Go deeper into the Word with practical teaching for everyday life.

Home Groups

Friday · 7:30 PM

Small groups that meet in the homes of leaders across different neighborhoods.

Youth Service

Saturday · 7:30 PM

Worship and teaching designed especially for teenagers and young adults.
